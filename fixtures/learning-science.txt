SYNTHETIC DEMO COURSE — Learning science fundamentals
This is invented educational sample material, not a university syllabus.

Retrieval practice means trying to recall an idea before looking at the answer. Checking notes after the attempt gives feedback about what is missing.
Spaced practice distributes review across separate sessions rather than one long session. For this sample course, compare what you recall before and after a break.
Interleaving mixes different problem types during practice. The learner must identify which approach fits rather than repeating one procedure without choosing.
A learning mistake log should state the attempted reasoning, describe the error, and record an actionable correction. A vague note such as 'study harder' is not a specific correction.
Practice feedback: confusing recognition with recall can lead a learner to overestimate readiness. Rereading a familiar explanation is not the same task as producing that explanation without notes.
No exam dates, grade weights, or instructor requirements are specified in this sample.
