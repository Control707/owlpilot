# OwlPilot

OwlPilot is a student's personal academic co-pilot for planning study and learning from feedback. Its initial experience uses student-supplied course materials rather than live university connections.

## Language

**Study loop**:
The student's cycle of choosing daily priorities, recording a mistake after grading, and using a personalized review guide to prepare for a better next attempt.

**Mistake log**:
The student's record of errors and feedback from already-graded work or ungraded practice, used to identify topics to revisit.

**Review guide**:
Personalized study support grounded in course materials and the student's mistake log, not a graded submission or an answer to a live assessment.

**Demo data**:
Fictional academic information used to demonstrate OwlPilot, not evidence about the student's real courses or progress.
